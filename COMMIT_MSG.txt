feat(ui): waveform player + live generation progress

Restore two affordances Gradio provided out of the box:
- AudioPlayer now renders a decoded waveform (peaks via Web Audio) with a
  played/unplayed fill, click + arrow-key seeking, alongside the native
  <audio> controls and download link.
- While a generation is in flight, AudioPlayer shows an indeterminate
  progress bar and a live elapsed-seconds timer instead of a bare spinner.

Wired `loading` through Custom Voice, Voice Design, Voice Clone, and the
Voice Library card so the progress UI appears wherever audio is produced.
